# Code of conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, criticise code and not people, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and publishing someone's private data are not accepted.

Report a problem privately to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md), or through
https://github.com/workflowcat/odoo-live/security/advisories/new with "Conduct" in the title. Reports
are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments and block people who break these rules.
